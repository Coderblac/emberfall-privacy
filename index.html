Emberfall Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 7, 2026

This policy explains what data the mobile game Emberfall (Android package com.bighitcoder.emberfall) collects, why, who it is shared with, how long it is kept, and how you can delete it. Emberfall is made by BigHitCoder ("we", "us").

Questions or requests: bighitcoder@gmail.com

Summary

  • You can play without an account. Your progress is then kept only on your device.
  • If you sign in with Google, we store your game progress, your player name and portrait, your scores and your friends so you can play on any device and compete with others.
  • The game shows optional rewarded ads from Google AdMob, which uses your device's advertising ID.
  • We never sell your data.
  • You can delete your account and its data at any time, in the game or by email (how to delete your account).

1. Data we collect

a) Stored on your device (everyone)

The game saves your progress on your device: levels and stars, coins, boosters, hearts, settings (sound, music, vibration), your chosen player name and portrait, daily quests, events and similar game state. If you are not signed in, this data stays on your device and is not sent to us. It is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

b) If you sign in with Google

Signing in is optional. When you sign in, we receive and store:

  • Account details from Google: a unique account identifier, your email address, and the name and profile picture link on your Google account. Your email address is used only to identify your account. It is never shown to other players.
  • Player profile: the player name you choose, the portrait you pick from the game's built-in set, a friend code, your total stars and your highest level.
  • Game save: your progress and in-game items (stars, coins, boosters, hearts, quests, events and restoration progress), so they sync across your devices.
  • Scores: your best score and stars on each level, used for leaderboards.
  • Social features: your friends list, friend requests, score challenges you send or receive, and your part in team boss raids.
  • Gameplay statistics: for each level attempt, the level number, whether you won, lost or quit, moves left, score and the time. We use these to balance level difficulty.
  • Reward and moderation records: rewards you have claimed, and, if an account breaks the rules (for example, an offensive name or cheating), a record of any restriction.

c) Purchases

Coin packs are sold through Google Play. Google processes the payment; we never see your card or payment details. To deliver your coins and prevent fraud, we receive and store the purchase token, order ID, product ID and purchase time, linked to your account if you are signed in.

d) Advertising

Emberfall offers optional rewarded ads (for example, watch an ad for extra moves). These are provided by Google AdMob, which may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads, to show and measure ads and prevent fraud. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, we ask for your consent through Google's consent form before ads are personalised, and you can change your choice any time from Ad privacy in the game's account panel. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. See how Google uses data from apps that use its services.

2. How we use your data

  • To run the game and keep your progress safe and in sync across devices.
  • To show leaderboards and let you add friends, send challenges and join raids.
  • To deliver purchases and verify they are genuine.
  • To show optional rewarded ads.
  • To balance level difficulty and improve the game.
  • To keep the game fair and safe: preventing cheating, fraud and abuse.

3. What other players can see

If you sign in, other players can see your player name, portrait, total stars, highest level, level scores and friend code (on leaderboards, friend lists, challenges and raids). They cannot see your email address or your Google account name.

4. Who we share data with

We do not sell or rent your personal data. We share it only with service providers that run parts of the game for us, under their own privacy and security terms:

  • Supabase: hosting, database and sign-in for signed-in players (privacy policy).
  • Google: Google Sign-In, Google Play billing, and AdMob ads with its consent platform (privacy policy).

We may also disclose data if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our players.

5. How long we keep data

  • Account data (profile, save, scores, friends, challenges, raids, gameplay statistics, reward records) is kept while your account exists, and deleted when you delete your account.
  • Purchase records are kept for accounting, tax and fraud prevention. When you delete your account they are unlinked from it.
  • Data on your device stays until you uninstall the app or clear its data.
  • Ad data is kept by Google according to its own policies.

6. Deleting your data

You can delete your account and all data linked to it at any time:

  • In the game: tap your portrait on the home screen, then Delete account. It takes effect immediately.
  • By email: write to bighitcoder@gmail.com from the email address of your Google account. We delete the account within 30 days.

Full details: Delete your Emberfall account.

7. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Our database lets each player read and change only their own private data, and access to our systems is restricted. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal data, and to object to or restrict how it is used. You can change your player name and portrait in the game and delete your account at any time. For anything else, email us and we will respond within 30 days. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

9. Children

Emberfall is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the game changes. The effective date at the top shows when it was last changed. Significant changes will also be announced in the game.
